MARKETING MIX

Greener only works if it is also cheaper and easier

🌍
PRODUCT · TRIPLE BOTTOM LINE

Planet, People, Profit

Fewer packs per serving; low-income buyers not priced out; packaging cost spread over many servings.

🏷️
PRICE · GOOD-VALUE + DEPOSIT

Rp2,460 per glass

5% below the Rp2,580 sachet. Starter kit bundle Rp49,900. Rp500 deposit refunded on return.

🚚
PLACE · GREEN CHANNEL

Forward and back on one truck

Omnichannel vertical system; Waste Stations, warung and waste banks as return points; backhaul of empties.

📣
PROMOTION · IMC

"Isi Energi, Kurangi Sampah"

School sports events, QR on every pack, "10 returns = 1 free glass". Claims only after third-party audit.
